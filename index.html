Summer Movie Club

Lights down, popcorn ready — join us for the best films of the summer.

Buy your own ticket(s). After the credits roll, we gather in the lobby for discussion. Most screenings are Wednesday evenings post-release, once the summer crowds have moved on. Kids welcome, if accompanied by an adult. Some movies are more kid-friendly than others.
